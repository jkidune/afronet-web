### What We Do — Enabling Africa's Organic & Agroecology Transition

**Approach:** Pan-African · Collaborative · Standards-Driven · Evidence-Based · Market-Linked

Our mission is to strengthen Africa's organic ecosystem—connecting NOAMs, farmer organizations, policymakers, researchers, and markets. We support the development and adoption of trusted standards (EAOPS and national), build institutional capacity, and translate evidence into field-use tools.

#### 01. Capacity & Networks
- **Focus:** Capacity building
- We strengthen NOAMs and farmer organizations with trainings and peer learning.

#### 02. Standards & Policy
- **Focus:** Organic standards
- We support the development and uptake of continental and national organic standards and guidance.

#### 03. Knowledge & Resources
- **Focus:** Practical toolkit
- We curate practical toolkits, research, and training for practitioners and decision makers.

#### 04. Markets & Partnerships
- **Focus:** Marketing
- We connect producers, processors, and private sector to grow fair, sustainable value chains.

### Our Programme — Integrated African Agricultural Business Alliance (IIABA)

IIABA drives sustainable agricultural transformation by connecting markets, ensuring quality assurance, and influencing public policy across Africa. We connect organic producers with regional and global market opportunities. We develop robust certification and participatory guarantee systems.

### 01. What We Do

AfrONet strengthens Africa's organic ecosystem from farm to policy to market. We build NOAM and farmer leadership, steward standards (EAOPS and national), translate evidence into practical tools, and foster fair, traceable value chains.

| Area | Description |
|---|---|
| Capacity & Networks | Governance support, training of trainers, and peer learning. |
| Standards & Policy | Practical guidance and multi-stakeholder consultations. |
| Knowledge & Resources | Toolkits, curricula, plain-language briefs, and data. |
| Market & Partnerships | Buyer readiness, quality systems, and linkages. |

---

### 02. Our Mission

To catalyse a distinctive Organic Agriculture sector that transforms smallholder agriculture into ecologically sustainable, socially and culturally inclusive, economically viable, efficient, and competitive systems—advancing food security and sovereignty while enabling income growth and equity.

### Help Scale Organic & Agroecology Across Africa

#### Why Your Support Matters

Your gift strengthens Africa's organic ecosystem so farmers, NOAMs, policymakers, researchers, and market actors can put proven practices to work.
