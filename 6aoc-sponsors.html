6AOC Sponsors & Partners

We gratefully acknowledge the following organisations for supporting the 6th African Organic Conference, Lusaka, 1–4 December 2026.

In Recognition

Partners of the 5th AOC (Kigali, 2023)

The previous conference was supported by: African Organic Network (AfrONet) · Government of the Republic of Rwanda · Rwanda Organic Agriculture Movement (ROAM) · Inter-continental Network of Organic Farmers Organization (INOFO) · Network of Organic Agriculture Researchers in Africa (NOARA) · Kenya Organic Agriculture Network (KOAN) · African Union Commission / EOA Continental Secretariat · Agence Française de Développement (AFD) · Research Institute of Organic Agriculture (FiBL) · Naturland Association for Organic Agriculture · SWISSAID · GIZ · IFOAM Organics International Asia · International Society for Organic Agriculture Research (ISOFAR) · and National Organic Agriculture Movements (NOAMs) across the continent.
