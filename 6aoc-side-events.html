Host a Side Event

Day 3 of the 6AOC is reserved for side events, business discourse, and field visits — a chance to go beyond the presentation tracks and see organic agriculture in action. Organisations, networks, development partners and other groups may apply to organise side events alongside the main conference programme. Side events provide an opportunity to convene targeted discussions, launch initiatives, share research, conduct stakeholder meetings or explore specific areas of organic agriculture and agroecology.

Organisations interested in hosting a side event can register at the Side Event rate listed on the Registration & Fees page.

Day 3

Field Visits

On Day 3, participants will visit selected sites in and around Lusaka showcasing organic agriculture in practice:

  • Renowned organic farms
  • Value-addition enterprises
  • Organic marketing facilities
  • Other organic agriculture initiatives in Zambia

Specific side-event listings and field-visit sites will be added here as they are confirmed.
