The Road to Zambia

The 5th AOC and the Road to Zambia

The 5th African Organic Conference, held in Kigali, Rwanda in December 2023 under the theme "Strengthening Resilient and Sustainable Food Systems in Africa through Organic Agriculture," brought together 300 attendees — farmers, scientists, policy makers, students and entrepreneurs — from across Africa, Europe, Asia and Oceania, both in person and virtually.

Delegates adopted the Kigali 2023 Declaration, a set of recommendations and calls to action for African governments, the African Union, regional bodies, and international partners on strengthening organic agriculture across the continent. The Declaration closed with the conference formally agreeing that Zambia is a candidate to host the 6th African Organic Conference in 2026, pending a duly filed application to the AfrONet Board — a candidacy since confirmed, with the Organic Producers' & Processors' Association of Zambia (OPPAZ) now hosting 6AOC in Lusaka.

In recognition of the 5th AOC's continuity with 6AOC, its core partners included: AfrONet, Government of Rwanda, ROAM, INOFO, NOARA, KOAN, AUC/EOA, AFD, FiBL, Naturland, SWISSAID, GIZ, IFOAM Organics International Asia, and ISOFAR — labeled here as 5th AOC partners, not confirmed 6AOC sponsors.
