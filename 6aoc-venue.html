Venue & Accommodation

Chongwe, Lusaka, Zambia

The 6th African Organic Conference will be held in Chongwe, Lusaka, Zambia, hosted by the Organic Producers' & Processors' Association of Zambia (OPPAZ).

The conference venue is Mika Convention Centre (MCC) in Chongwe. Recommended hotels near the venue, with indicative rates and contact details, are listed below under Where to Stay. Further travel guidance will be shared with registered participants.
