6th African Organic Conference (6th AOC)

It is now official. Following the resounding success of the 5th African Organic Conference, held in Kigali, Rwanda, from 12th to 15th December 2023, the 6th African Organic Conference is set to take place from 1st to 4th December 2026 in Lusaka, Zambia.

Mark your calendars, save the dates and plan to be part of this important continental event, which will bring together key stakeholders in the organic and agroecology sector across Africa and beyond. Keep an eye out and stay tuned for upcoming details and exciting updates ahead of the conference.
