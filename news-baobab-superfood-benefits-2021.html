The Benefits of Baobab Powder as a 100% Natural Organic Super-Food

Baobab is an exceptional super-fruit of Africa’s ‘Tree of Life’. It is the only fruit in the world that dries naturally on the branch. When baked in the sun for at least six months, baobab’s prickly green, furry coating transforms into a smooth, brown, coconut-like shell; inside its hard casing, the pulp is naturally dehydrated. It can be simply harvested and sieved to produce a 100 percent natural and organic super-food powder, exceptionally nutrient-rich with a wide range of health benefits.

Baobab powder is a rich source of vitamin C, which contributes to normal energy release, immune function, and healthy, glowing skin. It is also almost 50 percent fibre (two-thirds soluble, one-third insoluble, making it a powerful prebiotic) and contains more antioxidants than any other whole fruit. As a natural source of these nutrients, baobab is more bioavailable than manufactured vitamin supplements, meaning our bodies can absorb the nutrients more easily. Amazingly, baobab has a natural shelf-life of three years, with no additives or preservatives needed — just 100 percent pure baobab fruit pulp powder.

A single serving of Baobab Powder (10g, or 2-3 teaspoons) provides 33 percent of your daily vitamin C requirement. Vitamin C also helps the body absorb iron, so pairing iron-rich foods with baobab increases absorption — useful since, according to the World Health Organisation, over 30 percent of the world’s population was deficient in iron by 2017, making it the most common nutritional disorder. Baobab Powder contains 34 percent soluble fibre, which slows the release of sugars into the bloodstream, reducing energy spikes, helping control blood glucose and improving cholesterol.

A Secret to Gut Health

The soluble fibres of baobab fruit pulp are prebiotics — indigestible dietary fibre that beneficially stimulates the growth of beneficial gut micro-flora. Studies of the Hadza Tribe in Tanzania, among the world’s last remaining hunter-gatherers, found they have 40 percent more diverse gut microbiomes than the average Westerner — one of their key staples being the baobab fruit. The Journal of Nutrition also notes baobab has the highest antioxidant content of any fruit, with twice the antioxidants gram-for-gram of goji berries and more than blueberries and pomegranates combined. It is generally advised that pregnant women consume 85mg of vitamin C daily, which baobab can help provide, supporting collagen production and a baby’s developing organs, as well as increasing iron absorption critical to producing red blood cells.
