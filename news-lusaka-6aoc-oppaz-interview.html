Lusaka to Host 6th AOC as Africa's Organic Farming Movement Picks Up Steam

Come December 2026, all roads in Africa's organic farming world will lead to Lusaka. Zambia's capital will host the 6th African Organic Conference (6th AOC) from the 1st to the 4th of December. “That's a big deal for the country's organic agriculture sector, which has been growing quietly but steadily,” said Mushimbwe Chitalu, OPPAZ's Executive Director and Vice President of the African Organic Network (AfrONet), in an interview with AfrONet News recently.

The theme of the conference is: “Building up resilient agri-food systems and ensuring the right to food in Africa through organic farming.” He pointed out that across the continent, farmers are feeling the weight of climate change, and food insecurity remains a real challenge, adding that many are now turning to homegrown, nature-friendly solutions, and this event will shine a spotlight on what's working.

“Just like in past conferences, the program will be divided into four connected tracks: Farmers, Policy, Research, and Market & Trade,” affirmed Chitalu. Why four? Because the organisers know that real change doesn't happen in one corner alone — you need farmers sharing what they face on the ground, policymakers listening, researchers testing new ideas, and markets opening up. He emphasised that a special Farmers' Track will give smallholders, the real backbone of African agriculture, space to speak, compare notes and tackle practical problems. The idea is simple: don't talk about farmers without farmers in the room.

The African Organic Conference first began in Kampala, Uganda, back in 2009. Since then, it has grown into one of the continent's major gatherings, regularly pulling in over 300 participants. The 2026 edition is expected to attract an even wider mix: government officials, international NGOs, African Union and UN agencies, researchers, and private sector players, all coming together under one roof.

Zambia got the nod to host after Kenya stepped back from the race. The decision was confirmed during the 5th African Organic Conference in Kigali, Rwanda, in December 2023. On the final day of the four-day event, the African Organic Network (AfrONet) will hold its General Assembly — a session that matters, with elections for a new Governing Board that will shape where the movement heads next.

For many, the return to Lusaka feels like a homecoming, a reminder that Zambia remains committed to an agricultural path that is both organic and resilient. Full conference details, registration and the programme are available on AfrONet's dedicated 6th AOC event page; the earlier conference announcement can also be found here.
