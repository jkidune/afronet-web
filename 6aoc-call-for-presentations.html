Call for Presentations

  • Home
  • Call for Presentations
30 September 2026 Abstract submission deadline
31 October 2026 Full paper deadline (if abstract accepted)

AfrONet invites multi-stakeholder actors across organic agriculture value chains — in Africa and abroad — to submit presentations of their research, innovations, and success stories in Organic Agriculture and Agroecological practice.

Presentations should address sustainable production, food security and nutrition, resilient food systems, and the involvement of women, youth and other disadvantaged social groups in the face of a changing climate. Successful submissions will show clear knowledge, practices, attitudes, skills, and innovations — and concrete actions — for adoption, adaptation, or scaling up/out toward resilient agri-food systems and a solid agribusiness base spanning national, continental and international trade. Presentations on experiences from Africa that meet these criteria will be given priority.

Eligible participants — researchers, farmers, value-chain actors and others — should submit presentations of their research, innovations, or success stories in line with the guidelines below.

Please note: only participants duly registered for the conference are authorized to present papers or posters. All accepted manuscripts will be published in the Conference Proceedings; scientific papers judged suitable for journal publication may also be published in the African Journal of Organic Agriculture and Ecology (AJOAE).

Scope and Language

Contributions are welcome across Agricultural Economics, Agricultural Engineering, Agricultural Extension, Agroforestry, Agroecology, Animal Science, Crop/Environmental Protection, Crop Science/Horticulture, Environmental Sciences, Fishery/Aquaculture, Nutrition, Pharmacy, Soil Science, Veterinary Medicine, Wildlife Management, and other relevant fields.

Manuscripts may be submitted in English, French, or Portuguese.

How to Submit

Scientific papers

  • Abstract – up to 250 words – by 30 September 2026
  • Full paper (if abstract accepted) – by 31 October 2026

Submit to: africanorganicconference@gmail.com

Poster presentations
A one-page poster with name, location, and a description of the success story in no more than 30 words.

Farmer success stories
Five to ten slides of posters/pictures, or a 2-minute video clip, showcasing a specific organic agriculture success story, with a description of no more than 15 words.

Manuscript Guidelines (for authors of scientific papers)

Format

  • Times New Roman, 12pt, double-spaced for abstracts; 1.5-spaced for full manuscripts, A4 (210 × 297mm)
  • Maximum length: 10 pages, including tables, figures and illustrations
  • Margins: 2.54cm on all sides
  • Cover page: manuscript title; author name(s) and affiliation(s) with correspondence email and phone; abstract of ≤250 words with up to 5 keywords

Structure
Title → Abstract → Introduction → Aims/Objectives → Materials and Methods → Results → Discussion → Conclusion and Recommendations → References. Main headings centered, Sentence case; sub-headings left-aligned. Use SI units. Number tables/figures in Arabic numerals with a caption below each.

References (APA style)
Cited in-text by author name and year. Full reference list in alphabetical order by first author's surname. Example (journal): Lawale A. A. (1991). "A Descriptive Analysis of Agriculture Cooperative Model in Rural Development." Journal of Rural Development and Administration. 23(3):1-8.

Full formatting rules, including book and book-chapter citation formats, are in the downloadable Call for Presentations document below.
