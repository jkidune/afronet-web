Our Programmes

Driving institutional change
across Africa

AfrONet's programmes build the institutional foundations for a thriving organic sector — from policy and markets to certification and capacity.

What We Do

Four pillars of our work

Capacity & Networks

Strengthen NOAMs and farmer organizations with governance support, training of trainers, and peer learning.

Standards & Policy

Develop and roll out practical guidance aligned with EAOPS and national standards.

Knowledge & Resources

Create toolkits, briefings, and multilingual training materials for real-world use.

Markets & Partnerships

Build fair, traceable value chains that reward organic producers.

Our Programmes

Agroecology

Agroecology Promotion Programme (APP)

Timeline: May 2024 – March 2028

A multi-partner programme funded by the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation, strengthening Africa's organic movement through networks, policy dialogue, and youth and women empowerment.

View programme
Networking

African Organic Conference (AOC)

Timeline: Triennial (Every 3 Years)

The premier continental platform uniting organic agriculture actors every three years for policy dialogue, knowledge sharing, and trade facilitation.

View programme
Policy

Ecological Organic Agriculture Initiative (EOA-I)

Timeline: Ongoing

A continent-wide African Union Commission initiative mainstreaming ecological organic agriculture into national policies and agricultural programmes.

View programme
Innovation

IIABA Initiative (Institutional Innovations for Organic Agriculture)

Timeline: 2020–2023

A pioneering effort in Tanzania, Uganda, and Morocco to advance the institutional innovations needed for organic agriculture to thrive at scale.
