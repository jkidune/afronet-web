## Style
- French: sentence case for headings, French typography (space before « : ; ? ! », « guillemets »), "vous".
- Portuguese: European/African norm (as used in Mozambique and Angola), "você" avoided in favour of impersonal forms.
- Kiswahili: standard Kiswahili (Tanzania), formal register. Needs native-speaker review before launch.
- Dates: 1–4 décembre 2026 · 1–4 de dezembro de 2026 · 1–4 Desemba 2026.
