6AOC

Objectives

Participants will interact through the debate of paper presentations, product exhibitions, and field trips, to identify actions organic agriculture can take to build resilient agri-food systems and contribute positively to the right to food in Africa. The food systems envisioned embrace cultural and social sensitivity, fairness in access to the factors of production, and fairness in how benefits are shared. Adaptation and mitigation actions for climate change and other environmental pressures will also be considered — alongside the role of the private and public sectors in building partnerships with smallholder farmers, from farm to fork.

6 th
Chief Objective

To find solutions for ensuring food security and nutrition in Africa

Spanning the production and marketing of livestock, food, fibre and medicinal plants.

Specific Objectives

The Conference Will Specifically Seek To

1

Reflect & Progress

Convene a conference of organic stakeholders to discuss the developments, progress, and challenges encountered across Africa since the last AOC, held in Kigali.

2

Share the Evidence

Share information on the tangible benefits organic agriculture has delivered for national, regional and continental economies, human health, soil health, climate change mitigation, and resilience-building.

3

Drive Adoption

Strategize mechanisms for promoting the adoption of organic agriculture by stakeholders and communities across Africa.

4

Engage the Sectors

Motivate active private- and public-sector involvement in developing organic agriculture and institutionalizing it — through bodies including the AUC, RECs, UNFAO, UNCTAD, UNIDO, development partners, regional bodies, national governments, and the private sector.

5

Bridge the Concepts

Enhance understanding of the role of Agroecology within Organic Agriculture.

6

Harmonise & Integrate

Support the harmonization of approaches, regional integration, and intra-Africa collaboration and trade.

The 6th African Organic Conference — 1–4 December 2026, Lusaka, Zambia.
