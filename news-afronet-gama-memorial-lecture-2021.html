AfrONet Remembers Its First President with Deep Fondness

The African Organic Network (AfrONet) recently held a memorial public lecture in memory of its late president Jordan Gama, who passed away a year ago in Dar es Salaam. The virtual meeting was organized by close working colleagues of Mr Gama, who was also Chief Executive Officer of the Tanzania Organic Agriculture Movement (TOAM).

Among the speakers was Mr Gama’s successor as AfrONet president, Josephine Atangana, who delivered a moving tribute to her former boss covering the many years they worked together, with Jordan as the first AfrONet president and Josephine as vice-president. “It is a loss difficult to bear not only for the organic agriculture development community in Tanzania and Africa, but the world at large. In Jordan we lost a true expert, leader and friend, a humble and very kind man,” remarked Josephine.

She said Mr Gama was an avid supporter of smallholder farmers’ rights and always dreamed that one day they would graduate to medium-sized farmers with a highly-valued contribution to food security and sovereignty. “He was very kind to everyone, but very strict when it comes to work, a team builder who oversaw a very strong network of organic agriculture development in Tanzania and Africa.” Quoting Paul Valéry, she added: “A great man is one who leaves others at a loss after he is gone. I know we are all still suffering from the loss of this great man, but we can still celebrate his wonderful life.”

Jordan Gama passed away at 9am on August 19, 2020 at Hindu Mandal Hospital in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. He fell sick during an official trip to Njombe and Ruvuma regions in the southern highlands of Tanzania in mid-July 2020, and was rushed to the Ruvuma Referral Hospital in Songea municipality before being transferred to Hindu Mandal Hospital on August 7. The AfrONet governing board endorsed Josephine Atangana from Cameroon to succeed Mr Gama as the continental network’s leader.
