Our Impact

Real change across Africa's organic sector

AfrONet works strategically to transform organic agriculture across Africa. We collaborate with partners to create meaningful change — from continental policy dialogue to grassroots capacity building. Here's what that looks like in practice, told through our programmes, their outcomes, and the people we work with.

Featured Programmes

Agroecology · May 2024 – March 2028

Agroecology Promotion Programme (APP)

A multi-partner programme promoting agroecology and organic agriculture across parts of Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa and South-East Asia, funded by the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation (SDC).

27 paying members targeted · 16 NOAM small grants · Pan-African reach

View full case study →
Networking · Triennial

African Organic Conference (AOC)

The premier continental platform uniting organic agriculture actors across Africa and beyond — policy dialogue, knowledge sharing, and trade facilitation every three years.

5 conferences hosted · 300+ average attendees · Pan-African reach

View full case study →
Policy · Ongoing

Ecological Organic Agriculture Initiative (EOA-I)

A continent-wide mission driven by the African Union Commission to mainstream ecological organic agriculture into national policies and agricultural programmes across Africa.

15+ AUC steering meetings · NOAMs as primary beneficiaries · Continent-wide scope

View full case study →
Innovation · 2020–2023

IIABA Initiative

A pioneering effort to propel organic agriculture beyond production augmentation — bridging research institutions, grassroots movements, and federations across Tanzania, Uganda, and Morocco.

€1.5 million budget allocated · 3 pilot countries · 20+ roundtable discussions
