6th African Organic Conference

6th African Organic Conference — 6AOC

Building Resilient Agri-Food Systems for Africa

1–4 December 2026 Lusaka, Zambia Physical & Virtual Participation

The African Organic Network (AfrONet), in partnership with the Organic Producers' & Processors' Association of Zambia (OPPAZ), invites organic agriculture stakeholders from across Africa and beyond to the 6th African Organic Conference (6AOC) in Lusaka, Zambia. Held every three years, 6AOC brings together farmers, researchers, businesses, policymakers, development partners, civil society, students and other actors shaping the future of organic agriculture and agroecology in Africa.

About

About the Conference

The African Organic Conference is one of the continent's key platforms for dialogue, knowledge exchange, networking and collaboration within the organic agriculture sector. The 6th edition returns to Lusaka, Zambia, where the second African Organic Conference was held in 2012. The conference will be hosted by OPPAZ on behalf of AfrONet and will provide both physical and virtual participation opportunities. Over four days, participants will engage in presentations, exhibitions, business discussions, side events, field visits and the AfrONet General Assembly.

Conference Theme

Building up resilient agri-food systems and ensuring the right to food in Africa through organic farming

Organic agriculture combines indigenous knowledge with scientifically proven approaches to improve agricultural productivity while protecting soil, ecosystems, human health and communities. The conference will explore how organic farming and agroecology can contribute to:

  • Sustainable agricultural production
  • Food and nutrition security
  • Resilient food systems
  • Climate change adaptation and mitigation
  • Increased participation of women and youth
  • Inclusive agricultural development
  • Stronger organic value chains
  • Agribusiness development
  • Intra-African and international trade
  • Adoption and scaling of appropriate agricultural innovations

Participants will examine the knowledge, practices, technologies, policies, partnerships and investments needed to build stronger and more resilient African food systems.

Objectives

Conference Objectives

The main objective of the 6th African Organic Conference is to identify solutions for strengthening food security and nutrition in Africa through the production and marketing of organic livestock, food, fibre and medicinal plants. The conference will specifically seek to:

1

Reflect & Progress

Bring together organic agriculture stakeholders to discuss developments, progress and challenges in Africa since the 5th African Organic Conference in Kigali.

2

Share the Evidence

Share evidence on the contribution of organic agriculture to national, regional and continental economies, human health, soil health, climate change mitigation and resilience.

3

Drive Adoption

Develop strategies for increasing the adoption of organic agriculture among African communities and stakeholders.

4

Engage the Sectors

Encourage greater participation of the private and public sectors in developing and institutionalising organic agriculture.

5

Bridge the Concepts

Strengthen understanding of the relationship between agroecology and organic agriculture.

6

Harmonise & Integrate

Support harmonisation of approaches, regional integration, intra-African collaboration and trade.
