  • Conference Tracks

The 6AOC is organized around four tracks, each anchored by a lead organization representing a different part of Africa's organic agriculture community.

Led by INOFO Africa

Farmers' Track

Creates space for farmers and farmer organisations to share experiences, innovations, challenges and solutions from across the continent. Lead organization: Inter-Continental Network of Organic Farmer Organizations (INOFO – Africa).

Led by NOARA

Research Track

Researchers, academics and students share evidence, innovations and findings relevant to organic agriculture, agroecology and resilient food systems. Lead organization: Network of Organic Agriculture Researchers in Africa (NOARA).

Led by KOAN

Trade / Exhibition Track

Connects producers, processors, buyers, exporters, service providers and other value-chain actors, with opportunities for product and innovation showcases. Lead organization: Kenya Organic Agriculture Network (KOAN).

Led by AU Commission EOA-I

Policy Track

Brings together policymakers and stakeholders to discuss policies, strategies and institutional frameworks supporting organic agriculture and agroecology in Africa. Lead organization: African Union Commission Continental Secretariat of the Ecological Organic Agriculture (EOA) Initiative.

The 6th African Organic Conference — 1–4 December 2026, Lusaka, Zambia.
