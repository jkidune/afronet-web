About AfrONet

What is AfrONet and what do you do?

AfrONet is the African Organic Network, a continental umbrella organization founded in 2012. We unite and represent organic agriculture stakeholders across Africa through policy dialogue, capacity building, knowledge sharing, and trade facilitation.

Where does AfrONet operate?

AfrONet is headquartered in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, and works continentally across Africa through National Organic Agriculture Movements and partner organizations, with current pilot countries including Tanzania, Uganda, and Morocco.

How is AfrONet funded?

Our programmes are supported by international development partners, including the Agence Française de Développement (AFD) and the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation (SDC), alongside institutional and corporate partnerships. See our Support Us page for ways to give.

Membership & Getting Involved

How can my organization become a member?

National Organic Agriculture Movements (NOAMs), farmer organizations, and other organic agriculture stakeholders can reach out through our Contact page to start a membership conversation with our team in Dar es Salaam. See our Members & Partners page for the network AfrONet already works with.

I'm an individual, student, or researcher — can I get involved?

Yes. AfrONet's programmes and events — including the African Organic Conference — welcome researchers, students, and individual practitioners alongside institutional members. See the 6th African Organic Conference page for an upcoming opportunity to participate.

How can I partner with or support AfrONet?

We work with institutional, corporate, and development partners in a range of ways — co-funding programmes, sponsoring continental convenings, or supporting resource development. Visit Support Us for the ways to give, or contact us directly to discuss a partnership.

Research & Policy

Can I access your policy briefs and research?

Yes — our policy documents and research are shared through the Standards section on our homepage, and new findings are published through our News page.

What organic standards does AfrONet work with?

AfrONet supports the East African Organic Products Standard (EAOPS) and Participatory Guarantee Systems (PGS) — community-based organic certification approaches — alongside national organic standards across the countries we work in.
