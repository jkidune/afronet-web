Summary of the 6AOC Programme

  • 1–4 December 2026
  • Mika Convention Centre, Lusaka, Zambia

Theme: Building resilient agri-food systems and ensuring the right to food in Africa through organic agriculture

Day Date Key programme items
Day 1 1 December 2026 Inaugural session, Plenary I and Track Sessions Block 1
Day 2 2 December 2026 Plenary II and Track Sessions Blocks 2 & 3
Day 3 3 December 2026 Plenary III, Track Sessions Block 4, Side Events, Call to Action (Lusaka Declaration) and official closure of the 6th AOC
Day 4 4 December 2026 Field Excursion and AfrONet General Assembly (GA)
Day by Day

Programme of Events

  • 07:30 – 08:50Delegate Arrival and Registration
  • 08:50 – 10:30Inauguration Ceremony and VIP Opening
  • 10:30 – 11:00Refreshment Break, Group Photo and Press Briefing
  • 11:00 – 13:00Plenary I: Building Resilient Agri-Food Systems
  • 13:00 – 14:00Lunch Break
  • 14:00 – 16:00Track Sessions Block 1: Parallel Sessions (Tracks A, B, C, D)
  • 16:00 – 17:00Daily Track Synthesis Roundtable (Main Hall)
  • 17:00 onwardsWelcome Reception and Cultural Evening
Detailed programme — Day 1

07:30 – 08:50Delegate Arrival and Registration

08:50 – 10:30Inauguration Ceremony (Main Hall)

  • 08:50 – 09:15 Welcome Remarks (AfrONet President and OPPAZ Chair)
  • 09:15 – 09:35 Welcome Address (Minister of Agriculture, Zambia)
  • 09:35 – 10:00 Keynote Address and Official Opening (The Republican Vice President of Zambia)
  • 10:00 – 10:30 VIP Tour of Exhibition Booths and Poster Area

10:30 – 11:00Refreshment Break, Official Group Photo and Press Briefing

11:00 – 13:00Plenary I: Building Resilient Agri-Food Systems (Main Hall)

  • Moderator: Conference Planning Committee nominee (to be confirmed)
  • 11:00 – 11:10 Moderator’s Framing and Objectives
  • 11:10 – 11:30 Keynote 1 (Continental Framework): Implementing the Kampala CAADP Strategy and Action Plan (2026–2035): Implications for Organic Agriculture and Resilient African Agrifood Systems — African Union Commission representative (to be confirmed)
  • 11:30 – 11:50 Keynote 2 (Scientific Evidence): Applied Research and Agroecological Innovations — ASARECA / ISOFAR / FARA representative (to be confirmed)
  • 11:50 – 12:10 Keynote 3 (Grassroots Action): Farmer-Led Seed Systems and Production Resilience — INOFO representative (to be confirmed)
  • 12:10 – 12:50 Moderated Panel Discussion and Audience Q&A
  • 12:50 – 13:00 Synthesis of Key Recommendations for the Declaration Drafting Team

13:00 – 14:00Lunch Break and Poster Viewing

14:00 – 16:00Track Sessions Block 1 (Concurrent Thematic Rooms – 120-minute block)

  • Format: 1 lead presentation (20 min) + 4 selected papers (10 min each) + moderated Q&A (40 min) + transition and written synthesis (20 min)
  • Track A (Farmers – INOFO): Subtheme 1.1: Scaling climate-resilient organic production systems integrating indigenous farming knowledge
  • Track B (Research – NOARA): Subtheme 2.1: Empirical evidence on organic agriculture and agroecology for soil health and yield resilience
  • Track C (Value Chain – KOAN): Subtheme 3.1: Market Access and Intra-African Trade under AfCFTA
  • Track D (Policy – AU Commission EOA-I Initiative): Subtheme 4.1: Continental Policy Frameworks and Kampala CAADP Alignment

16:00 – 17:00Daily Track Synthesis Roundtable (Main Hall)

  • Moderator: Chair, Lusaka Declaration Task Force
  • Format: Rapid 5-minute briefings from the rapporteurs of Tracks A, B, C and D, followed by a moderated cross-track consensus debate to feed the overnight declaration draft

17:00 onwardsWelcome Reception and Cultural Evening

Day 2Deep Dive into Evidence and EconomicsWednesday, 2 December 2026
  • 08:30 – 09:00Welcome Coffee and Delegate Networking
  • 09:00 – 11:00Plenary II: Policy Experiences and Continental Standards
  • 11:00 – 11:30Refreshment Break and Structured B2B Exhibition
  • 11:30 – 13:00Track Sessions Block 2: Parallel Sessions (Tracks A, B, C, D)
  • 13:00 – 14:00Lunch Break
  • 14:00 – 16:00Track Sessions Block 3: Parallel Sessions (Tracks A, B, C, D)
  • 16:00 – 17:00Daily Track Synthesis Roundtable (Main Hall)
  • 17:00 onwardsFree Evening and Structured B2B Matchmaking
Detailed programme — Day 2

08:30 – 09:00Welcome Coffee and Delegate Networking

09:00 – 11:00Plenary II: Policy Experiences and Continental Standards (Main Hall)

  • Moderator: AfrONet Executive (to be confirmed)
  • 09:00 – 09:10 Moderator’s Framing and Introductions
  • 09:10 – 09:30 Keynote 1: Regional Harmonization of Organic Standards and Verification — ARSO / REC representative (to be confirmed)
  • 09:30 – 09:50 Keynote 2: Mobilizing Public Procurement and Innovative Agribusiness Finance — IFAD / AfDB representative (to be confirmed)
  • 09:50 – 10:10 Keynote 3: Mainstreaming EOA into National Agricultural Investment Plans — Host Government / Policy Lab (to be confirmed)
  • 10:10 – 10:50 Moderated Panel Debate and Audience Q&A
  • 10:50 – 11:00 Synthesis and Declaration Action Points

11:00 – 11:30Refreshment Break and Structured B2B Exhibition Matchmaking

11:30 – 13:00Track Sessions Block 2 (Concurrent Thematic Rooms – 90-minute block)

  • Format: 1 lead presentation (15 min) + 3 selected papers (10 min each) + moderated Q&A (30 min) + transition and synthesis (15 min)
  • Track A (Farmers): Subtheme 1.2: Empowering women and youth: grassroots innovations and rural agribusiness
  • Track B (Research): Subtheme 2.2: Bridging the gap: translating research into farmer-centred innovations
  • Track C (Value Chain): Subtheme 3.2: Value Addition, Processing, and Post-Harvest Technologies
  • Track D (Policy): Subtheme 4.2: Regional Regulatory Harmonization and Input Validation Protocols

13:00 – 14:00Lunch Break and Exhibition Walkthrough

14:00 – 16:00Track Sessions Block 3 (Concurrent Thematic Rooms – 120-minute block)

  • Format: 1 lead presentation (20 min) + 4 selected papers (10 min each) + moderated Q&A (40 min) + transition and synthesis (20 min)
  • Track A (Farmers): Subtheme 1.3: Farmer-led adaptation strategies for climate change and biodiversity loss
  • Track B (Research): Subthemes 2.3 and 2.4: Socio-economic impacts, True Cost Accounting, and planetary health
  • Track C (Value Chain): Subtheme 3.3: Conformity Assessment, Participatory Guarantee Systems (PGS), and Traceability
  • Track D (Policy): Subthemes 4.3 and 4.4: Institutional capacity development and organic mechanization incentives

16:00 – 17:00Daily Track Synthesis Roundtable (Main Hall)

  • Focus: Consolidating Day 2 technical recommendations for integration into the 2nd Working Draft of the Lusaka Declaration

17:00 onwardsFree Evening and Dedicated B2B Deal-Rooms

Day 3Market Integration and Continental ResolutionsThursday, 3 December 2026
  • 08:30 – 09:00Welcome Coffee and Delegate Networking
  • 09:00 – 11:00Plenary III: Marketing, Trade, and Value Addition
  • 11:00 – 11:30Refreshment Break and Structured B2B Exhibition
  • 11:30 – 13:00Track Sessions Block 4: Parallel Sessions (Tracks A, B, C, D)
  • 13:00 – 14:00Lunch Break
  • 14:00 – 15:30Side Events and Regional Network Meetings
  • 15:30 – 17:00Official Closing Ceremony and Valedictory Session
    • Presentation and Adoption of the “Lusaka Declaration”
    • Closing Address by the Minister of Agriculture, Zambia
    • Official Voting Procedure and Announcement of the 7th AOC Host Country
  • 17:00 onwardsPublic Conference Programme Concludes
Detailed programme — Day 3

08:30 – 09:00Welcome Coffee and Delegate Networking

09:00 – 11:00Plenary III: Marketing, Trade, and Value Addition (Main Hall)

  • Moderator: KOAN / Trade Lead representative (to be confirmed)
  • 09:00 – 09:10 Moderator’s Framing and Introductions
  • 09:10 – 09:30 Keynote 1: De-risking Private Sector Investment in Commercial Organic Value Chains — Private agribusiness CEO (to be confirmed)
  • 09:30 – 09:50 Keynote 2: Digital Marketplaces, Regional Trade Corridors, and Non-Tariff Barriers — ITC / UNCTAD trade specialist (to be confirmed)
  • 09:50 – 10:10 Keynote 3: Youth and Women Leadership in Agri-Enterprise — Youth / women agripreneur representative (to be confirmed)
  • 10:10 – 10:50 Moderated Panel Debate and Audience Q&A
  • 10:50 – 11:00 Final Plenary Synthesis and Wrap-Up

11:00 – 11:30Refreshment Break and Final B2B Engagements

11:30 – 13:00Track Sessions Block 4 (Concurrent Thematic Rooms – 90-minute concluding block)

  • Focus: Concluding presentations, pedometrics/data systems (Research Track 2.5), inclusive business models (Value Chain Track 3.4/3.5), and final track resolutions sign-off

13:00 – 14:00Lunch Break

14:00 – 15:30Side Events and Regional Network Working Sessions

  • AfrONet Regional Clusters (East, West, Central, North and Southern Africa), NOARA Council, INOFO General Assembly preparation, and partner roundtables
  • Note: The Lusaka Declaration Task Force finalizes the official declaration text during this window.

15:30 – 17:00Official Closing Ceremony and Valedictory Session (Main Hall)

  • 15:30 – 16:00 Formal Presentation and Adoption of the “Lusaka Declaration” (Task Force Lead / AfrONet Secretariat)
  • 16:00 – 16:30 Valedictory Address by the Guest of Honour (Hon. Minister of Agriculture, Zambia)
  • 16:30 – 16:50 Formal Bidding, Member Voting, and Official Announcement of the 7th AOC Host Country
  • 16:50 – 17:00 Vote of Thanks by the Local Organizing Committee (LOC Chair)

17:00 onwardsPublic Conference Programme Concludes and Departures for Non-Voting Delegates

Day 4Field Context and Continental GovernanceFriday, 4 December 2026
  • 07:30 – 13:00Continental Field Visits (Excursions to PGS initiatives, organic farms and processing hubs)
  • 13:00 – 14:00Return to Venue and Lunch Break
  • 14:00 – 18:30AfrONet General Assembly (Closed Statutory Governance Session)
  • 18:30 onwardsGeneral Assembly Adjournment and Farewell Dinner
Detailed programme — Day 4

07:30 – 13:00Continental Field Learning Excursions

  • Operational routes: Pre-assigned shuttle departures to designated Lusaka-region certified organic estates, PGS smallholder cluster hubs, and value-addition/processing plants. Return transit is coordinated to arrive by 13:00.

13:00 – 14:00Return to Venue and Lunch Break

14:00 – 18:30AfrONet General Assembly (Closed Statutory Governance Session)

  • Location: Dedicated Executive Boardroom
  • Access: Restricted to registered AfrONet Board, National Organic Agriculture Movements (NOAMs), Regional Movements (ROAMs), and verified institutional voting delegates
  • Statutory agenda: Secretariat Activities Report, Audited Financial Statements, Approval of Motions, Election of the Continental Board, and Consideration of the 2027–2030 Strategic Framework

18:30 onwardsGeneral Assembly Adjournment

Open “Detailed programme” under each day for keynotes, track subthemes and session formats. Speakers and moderators will be confirmed closer to the conference.
