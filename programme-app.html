Agroecology Promotion Programme (APP)

The Agroecology Promotion Programme (APP) is a multi-partner programme promoting agroecology, organic agriculture, ecological organic agriculture and other like-minded approaches as pathways for transforming food and farming systems. The programme is funded by the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation and implemented across parts of Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa and South-East Asia.

In Sub-Saharan Africa, Biovision Africa Trust serves as the lead partner, while AfrONet participates as a subgrantee with a continental mandate to strengthen Africa's organic movement. Through APP, AfrONet supports stronger networks, policy dialogue, youth and women empowerment, institutional sustainability, research and knowledge sharing, and organic value chain development. The programme aims to build a stronger, more united and resilient continental organic movement that can engage governments, private sector actors and civil society in advancing the organic agriculture and agroecology agenda in Africa.

Impact & Objectives

Network Strengthening

Broaden AfrONet's membership base and strengthen collaboration with National Organic Agriculture Movements across Africa.

Youth & Women Empowerment

Support youth and women to take a more active role in shaping and driving AfrONet's continental agroecology agenda.

Policy Dialogue

Promote the mainstreaming of organic agriculture and agroecology into national, regional and continental policies, strategies and programmes.
